TW: Pregnancy, Infertility Treatment, TTC

A friend's pregnancy announcement, while a joyful event for many, can often evoke a spectrum of complex emotions for various reasons. Especially for those trying to conceive (TTC) or who have experienced pregnancy loss, it can trigger deep emotional pain. In this article, we emphasize "Your feelings are valid" and explore psychological approaches and practical strategies to protect your emotional well-being without guilt.

## It's Natural to Feel Hurt by a Friend's Pregnancy Announcement

When a friend shares their pregnancy news, you might feel guilty for not being able to celebrate wholeheartedly. However, experiencing a mix of emotions like jealousy, grief, sadness, feeling left behind, or even anger is completely natural. These feelings are rooted in your inner desires and experiences.

## What is Recurrent Pregnancy Loss (RPL)? Definition & Context

Recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL) is defined as experiencing two or more consecutive pregnancy losses. While previously defined as three or more, the recent trend is to consider two or more losses. For many couples in the TTC community, repeatedly facing losses without a clear explanation can be an immense emotional and physical burden. Your grief and frustration are completely understood.

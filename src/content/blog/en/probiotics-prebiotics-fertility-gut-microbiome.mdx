## The Link Between Gut Microbiome and Reproductive Function: Current Research Status

Our bodies host countless symbiotic bacteria, especially in the gut, where a diverse ecosystem of bacteria thrives. Recent research **suggests** that the balance of this gut microbiome **may** influence reproductive functions, such as ovarian function, embryo implantation, and pregnancy maintenance.

### What are Probiotics?

Probiotics are live microorganisms that, when administered in adequate amounts, confer a health benefit on the host. Lactic acid bacteria and bifidobacteria found in yogurt and fermented milk drinks are well-known examples. They are thought to contribute to improving digestion and regulating immune function by balancing the gut environment.
